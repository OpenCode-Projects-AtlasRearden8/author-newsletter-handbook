# Subject-Line Cheat Sheet

> 25 worked subject lines with the preheader each deserves. Rules of the
> craft live in [chapter 5](../docs/05-subject-lines.md); this is the
> deck to riff from. Replace the bracketed specifics with your own
> honest specifics -- the shape only works when the specificity is
> genuinely yours.

## The 25, grouped by formula

### The specific question

1. `Why does no one comment on indie books?` / Preheader: "The parts of
   book marketing that happen after the book is good."
2. `What would you do with 10 free subscribers?` / Preheader: "A startup
   plan so small you cannot talk yourself out of it."
3. `Is the free tier actually free?` / Preheader: "What each provider's
   fine print quietly expects from you."
4. `Could you set up a newsletter in one sitting?` / Preheader: "Ninety
   minutes, every finicky setting, never again."
5. `What do replies do that opens don't?` / Preheader: "Why the reply is
   the whole metric worth wanting."

### The numbered promise

6. `3 welcome emails you can steal today` / Preheader: "Copy, change the
   brackets, done in 15 minutes."
7. `2 settings authors never touch (and should)` / Preheader: "Reply-to
   and the From name -- the two cheapest upgrades in email."
8. `5 canned phrases to delete on sight` / Preheader: "The AI-draft
   pass, and what lands in the banned list."
9. `1 test to run before you pick a provider` / Preheader: "The exit
   door: export the list, export the mail, or walk away."
10. `4 emails that greet new readers for you` / Preheader: "The welcome
    sequence, written once, working forever."

### The confession (only when true)

11. `I broke the only rule I asked you to follow` / Preheader: "Month
    three, the monthly promise, and what I owe the list."
12. `The free tool I recommended, then un-recommended` / Preheader: "What
    changed in the terms, and the replacement I found."
13. `My first issue went to 12 people` / Preheader: "On embarrassment,
    and why 11 readers is secretly a fine start."

### The plain announcement

14. `Issue #1: plain-English AI, once a month` / Preheader: "What this
    is, why it exists, and the archive door."
15. `Chapter 2 explained like a friend would: free email providers` /
    Preheader: "The honest free tiers, with catches stated plain."
16. `New guide: the [30/90]-issue calendar, filled in` / Preheader: "A
    year of sends planned so future-you never stalls."

### The named series

17. `Toolkit Tuesday: free landing pages, ranked honestly` / Preheader:
    "What each free tier includes, and the catch, every time."
18. `Field notes: the 20-minute monthly ritual` / Preheader: "Export,
    save, one stats line -- the archive chapter, as a checklist."

### The question-personal

19. `What should the glossary define next?` / Preheader: "One question,
    replies get steered into real chapters."
20. `Did the checklist feel too small?` / Preheader: "On the minimum
    lovable issue, and what you'd cut."

### The curiosity-with-an-anchor (handles with care)

21. `The one setting that stops the spam folder` / Preheader: "SPF, DKIM,
    DMARC -- without the alphabet soup."
22. `Your reply-to address is doing more work than you think` /
    Preheader: "It decides where a reply lands, and replies are the
    cheapest growth tool."
23. *deliberately left blank -- see the note below on novelty bait
    before inventing "clever" lines.*
24. `Everything I send, archived on your hard drive` / Preheader: "Local
    files beat landlord platforms, and the ritual is 20 minutes."
25. `What 108 readers is actually worth` / Preheader: "Why reliable deep
    connection beats fluctuating reach, with arithmetic."

> A note on line 23: it is left blank on purpose. Novelty bait without
> an anchor -- a specific noun a reader recognizes -- is exactly the
> kind of cleverness chapter 5 of this handbook warns spends trust.
> If you cannot supply the anchor, skip the line, not the anchor.

## Usage notes

- Works best when the *issue* earns the line, not the reverse: write the
  issue, then pick from or riff on this deck (chapter 5's rule 5).
- Track what you sent in [the issue calendar](issue-calendar.csv)'s
  subject column, and check trends against replies rather than opens.
- The preheader's job is the payoff, not the echo. If the preheader only
  repeats the subject, delete it and write the issue's second sentence.
